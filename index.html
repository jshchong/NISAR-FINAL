How to read this

Each coloured pixel is a rice field whose growth stage was estimated from the NISAR L-band radar time series (HH and HV backscatter). Young, flooded paddies look dark to radar; backscatter rises as the canopy grows and levels off near maturity.

Use the timeline at the bottom to step through NISAR's 12-day repeat cycles, or press play. Switch to Radar to see the raw false-colour backscatter behind the stage map. A region with no pass in a cycle shows its previous image, dimmed. NISAR had an outage from 27 Jul to 10 Aug 2026, so cycles 26 and 27 are incomplete.

Planting timing compares each field's estimated planting date with the region's recommended planting window: blue on time, teal early, magenta late, grey planted before our first image, pale yellow not yet planted while the window is still open, dark purple no planting detected after the window closed. The Java and MADA (Kedah) windows are still open, so many of their fields show as not yet planted. Windows for 11 of 14 regions are typical calendars; only MADA, An Giang and Thai Binh use published 2026 schedules.

Calendar check = whether this region's mix of growth stages fits its local crop calendar on that date. It is a manual plausibility check by the team, not a measured accuracy; no field validation yet.

These are first estimates and have not been field-validated. Click inside an outlined region for its details.
